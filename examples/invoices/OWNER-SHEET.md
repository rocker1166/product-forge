## Left out on purpose, touching money, law or data

## Ten random "no" answers

- Customer, reverse: A master has nothing to refund or cancel; mistakes are edited, or the record is merged or archived.
- Customer, preview: There is no receiver view of a master record; previews belong to documents and content.
- Invoice, merge: Two issued documents are never joined; reverse one and make a new one.
- Customer, share link: Outsiders see documents or a portal, never the raw master record.
- Customer, schedule: Dates belong to documents and reminders, not to the master record itself.
