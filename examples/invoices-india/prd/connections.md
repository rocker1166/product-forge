# Invoices for Indian service firms: Connections

| Connection | Area | When | What | Set up | Test | When broken | When off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| login | signin | now | email and password sign-in, 10 characters or more, with TOTP two-step that an owner can require for all users; phone code, passkey and Google sign-in later | owner signs up with email, confirms it, may enrol an authenticator app by typing one valid code | sign in, fail five times, check the lock and the session list | reset link by email; owner can end any session | last active owner can never lose sign-in; TOTP can be switched off only by an owner; the sole owner who lost the phone uses one of 8 one-time recovery codes given at enrolment, or support resets the second step after the email and the GSTIN are proved, a 48-hour wait and a mail to every owner |
| phone_otp | signin | later | phone code sign-in waits for a registered SMS sender and templates plus SIM-swap rules |  |  |  |  |
| support_access | signin | now | support staff enter a firm only through an owner-granted read-only window of 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days, logged and shown in a banner | owner presses Allow support in Settings and picks 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days | grant, open as support, see the banner and the history row | expired grant shows no data and says why | no grant means no support view; nothing is exposed |
| upi_pay | payments | now | UPI QR and Pay with UPI button on the share page and PDF, plus I have paid claims; money is recorded by hand; the UPI ID and bank change takes effect after 24 hours (R62) | owner types the firm's UPI ID once, an owner-only field that emails all owners | open the share page on a phone and scan from another phone | page still shows the bank block and the claim button | no UPI ID means no QR and the bank block only |

| email | messaging | now | email from our verified domain with the firm's name as sender and the firm's address as Reply-To, queued through the outbox with retries | owner confirms the reply-to address by a verification email; the domain has SPF, DKIM and DMARC | send an invoice to yourself and to an address ending @fail.test | outbox shows failed with the reason and Retry; hard bounce suppresses the address | copy the share link or download the PDF by hand |
| whatsapp_link | messaging | now | WhatsApp click-to-chat with the customer's number, a short message and the share link filled in; the person taps Send | customer phone saved once, asked on first use | tap the button and see the prefilled chat | button asks for the phone; a blank PDF is never attached | email or Copy link |
