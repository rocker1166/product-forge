# Invoices for Indian service firms: product requirements

Printed from the map. Change the map, not this file. Filled.

Invoices and payments for small Indian service firms.

| Brief |  |
|---|---|
| who | the owner of a service firm of 1 to 20 people (design, IT, legal, consulting, agencies, repair, coaching), who also does the billing |
| job | send a correct GST invoice fast, get paid on time, and hand clean books to the accountant each month |
| country | India first (GST, e-invoice, TDS, DPDP, UPI); exports of services in foreign currency |
| money | monthly or yearly plan per firm, a free plan with a small limit, a trial |
| tenants | true |
| custom_objects | false |
| must_never | change an issued invoice, reuse or skip an invoice number, show one firm's data to another, lose a firm's books when it stops paying, send a wrong tax without saying so |
| size | L |

## Roles

| Role | Home | How they join | When they leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | home | signs up with email and a password; sign-up makes the firm and its first owner, or an owner hands the role over and the new owner accepts | hands ownership over first; the last owner cannot leave or close the login; the firm, its books and drafts stay |
| Accountant | home | by an owner's invite that expires in 7 days; a CA who already has a login accepts it and the firm shows in the switcher; or the CA creates the firm for a client and presses Hand to owner when it is set up | loses access on the next request when an owner removes them or they leave; their entries keep their name and their drafts stay with the firm |
| Staff | home | by an owner's invite that expires in 7 days; the invite page defaults to this role and says so | loses access on the next request when an owner removes them or they leave; drafts they made stay with the firm under their name |
| Viewer | home | by an owner's invite that expires in 7 days, for a partner or advisor who only reads | loses access on the next request when an owner removes them or they leave; they own no work, so nothing moves |
| Client | shared_document | opens a link from an invoice, credit note or quote mail or a WhatsApp tap and sees one document with no sign-in; or opens the client page link the firm sent for that customer and signs in with the password the firm set or sent, one password per customer, and sees that customer's documents, receipts and statement | the document link expires, is revoked, or the sender voids the document; a later send renews an expired link on an unpaid document. The client page ends after 2 idle hours, when the firm switches it off, or when five wrong passwords lock it for 30 minutes |
| Support | support_console | only when an owner grants access for 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days with a ticket number; read only, banner shown to the firm, every view logged with a Support label | the grant ends by its date or the owner ends it; sessions on that firm close at once |

## Objects

| Object | Kinds | Module | Why | Linked to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Allocation | money | collections | the split of one payment across the invoices it settles, so every rupee received points at an invoice | payment (never removed; a reversed payment turns its allocations reversed); invoice (never removed; an invoice with allocations cannot be voided) |
| Customer Credit | money | collections | the account of credit a customer holds with the firm, from credit notes, that can be applied to invoices or refunded | customer (a customer with a credit account cannot be deleted; archive instead); credit_note (a credit note whose credit was applied or refunded cannot be voided); refund (never removed; a reversed refund puts the amount back as a new row); invoice (never removed; an applied row keeps pointing at its invoice) |
| Credit Note | document, money | billing | the only way to lower an issued invoice; linked to one invoice, numbered in its own gapless series, and any excess becomes customer credit | invoice (an invoice with a credit note cannot be voided until that credit note is voided; the link never breaks); credit_ledger (ledger rows made from it stay; voiding the note is refused while any of its credit is spent or refunded); einvoice (the record stays for good); customer (keeps its own snapshot; the customer is archived, not deleted) |
| Customer | master | masters | the business or person a firm bills; its facts are copied onto every document when it is issued | invoice (blocked while any invoice exists, issued or void; archive instead. A customer with only drafts or nothing may be deleted after a confirm, and its drafts go with it.); quote (blocked while any quote exists; archive instead); payment (blocked while any payment exists; archive instead); credit_ledger (blocked while any credit row exists, even at zero balance; archive instead) |
| E-invoice Record | document, connection | tax | the portal side of one invoice or credit note: IRN, acknowledgement, signed QR and every attempt, behind one provider adapter | invoice (the record stays for good; voiding the invoice before any IRN closes it as cancelled); credit_note (the record stays for good; voiding the credit note before any IRN closes it as cancelled) |
| Expense | master | billing | money a firm spent for a customer and bills back on an invoice line. MAYBE WAVE 1, the owner decides before the build (question Q5); every answer below is marked room until then | customer (blocked while any expense is billable to the customer; archive the customer instead); invoice (a billed expense points at the invoice that holds its line; voiding the invoice or deleting its draft returns it to billable) |
| Export Job | job, file, report | migration | builds registers, the month-end accountant pack, Tally XML and the full-firm export as files; a link lives 7 days and the record stays | firm (records stay for the 8 year book-keeping period, then go with the firm); invoice (the file holds copies; invoices are never removed once issued); payment (the file holds copies; reversed payments stay listed as reversed) |
| Firm | master, setting | core | the tenant root; one service firm with its tax identity, defaults, lock date and the state of its books | user (a firm is never deleted while it holds issued documents; closing keeps books at least 8 years); membership (memberships go inactive with the firm; the names stay on history); history_entry (history stays at least 8 years after the firm is closed) |
| History Entry | other | core | the append-only record of every create, edit, issue, void, payment, reversal, sign-in and export | firm (history stays at least 8 years after the firm is closed); user (the actor's name stays as written when the login is closed); membership (role changes stay as written) |
| Import Job | job, file | migration | brings a firm's customers, services and old unpaid invoices in from a CSV or Excel file, checked first and imported whole or not at all | customer (rows made by a run belong to the customer; archiving a customer never removes the run's record); item (same as customers; the run record keeps its counts); invoice (opening invoices are never deleted once touched; the run keeps its counts and the history row) |
| Invite | person | core | a pending offer to join one firm in one role, usable only by the invited email | firm (invites end with the firm closing; they show as revoked); user (an invite is bound to an email, not to a login); membership (accepting makes or wakes the membership; the invite row stays as proof) |
| Invoice | document, money | billing | the legal tax paper a small Indian firm sends to be paid; frozen at issue, numbered without gaps, and settled through payments, credit notes and write-off | customer (the issued copy keeps its own snapshot of name, address, GSTIN and state; a customer with any invoice cannot be deleted, only archived); payment (a payment is never deleted; a reversed one stays listed and the invoice balance is recalculated); credit_note (issued credit notes stay linked for good; draft credit notes must be deleted or issued before the invoice can be voided); einvoice (the record stays for good; voiding an invoice before any IRN closes the record as cancelled); share_link (the link stays and shows a plain cancelled page after a void; it is never deleted); message (sent emails stay in the outbox log; nothing about the invoice can erase them); quote (deleting a draft made from a quote frees the quote again; an issued invoice keeps the quote number as its reference); recurring_plan (ending or stopping a plan leaves every invoice it made untouched) |
| Service Item | master | masters | a saved service whose SAC, unit, rate and tax rate are copied into an invoice line | invoice (blocked once any document line used it, and the screen says how many documents; archive instead. Lines keep their own copy either way.); quote (counted with invoices; any quote line that used it blocks delete); rate_master (the rate master row is never deleted; an item keeps the SAC and tax rate it was saved with and shows a warning when the table moves on) |
| LUT | setting | tax | the Letter of Undertaking that lets a firm issue export invoices without paying IGST; its number is read and printed on every such invoice | invoice (blocked once any invoice used it; it expires instead and invoices keep the printed LUT number); firm (belongs to the firm; if the firm is deleted it goes with it after the legal keep period) |
| Membership | person, setting | core | the role of one login inside one firm; the unit every 403 test runs against | firm (memberships go inactive when the firm is sealed; rows stay for history); user (a closed login makes its memberships inactive; rows stay) |
| Outbox Message | conversation | comms | every email the product sends for a firm, kept with its body, attachment, provider answer and what happened to it | invoice (the row stays; it names the document number it carried); customer (customers with documents are archived, never removed, so the row stays readable); invite (the row stays; the invite may expire but the sent mail is history); share_link (the row stays; the link inside it is revoked or expired, never erased); template (the row keeps the template version it was built from) |
| Number Series | setting | tax | the gapless counter, by firm, GSTIN, document type, prefix and financial year, that gives each document its number | invoice (a series is never deleted once any number was taken from it; it closes instead and its documents keep their numbers); credit_note (same: a series with any credit note is closed, not deleted); payment (same for receipt numbers); quote (same for quote and proforma numbers) |
| Payment | money | collections | money a customer paid against invoices, by hand or as an I-have-paid claim, with TDS and a numbered receipt | invoice (never removed; the payment stays on the invoice's page even when the invoice is later credited); allocation (allocations are never removed; a reversal turns them reversed); customer (a customer with a payment cannot be deleted; archive instead); number_series (the receipt counter is never reset or removed while a receipt exists) |
| Quote | document | sales | the offer or proforma a firm sends before work starts; it carries its own number but is never a tax document | customer (the quote keeps its own snapshot of name and address; a customer with quotes is archived, not deleted); invoice (the quote and the draft it made point at each other; deleting that draft frees the quote again, an issued invoice locks the link for good); share_link (the link stays and says expired or cancelled; it is never deleted) |
| Tax Master | master, setting | tax | the dated tables of GST rates, SAC codes, TDS sections, thresholds and state codes that every invoice and payment reads; the product owns it, no firm edits it | invoice (a row is never deleted while any invoice or line points to it; a newer row closes it); payment (a row is never deleted while any payment recorded a TDS rate from it); item (a row is never deleted; items keep the SAC and rate they were saved with) |
| Recurring Plan | job, setting | sales | a monthly, quarterly or yearly schedule that makes a review draft once per period and never issues it | customer (an archived customer makes a run fail with a reason and pauses the plan; the plan is kept); invoice (drafts and invoices it made stay untouched when the plan changes, stops or is deleted; a hard-deleted draft frees its period for one redo) |
| Refund | money | collections | cash the firm gave back out of a customer's credit, numbered REF and never above the balance | credit_ledger (never removed; a reversal writes the amount back as a new credit row); customer (a customer with a refund cannot be deleted; archive instead); number_series (the REF counter is never reset or removed while a refund exists) |
| Share Link | connection | comms | the private web page where a customer reads one invoice, quote or credit note, pays by UPI and downloads the PDF without a login | invoice (invoices are never removed once issued; the link stays and says voided); quote (a quote link stays and shows its final state); credit_note (credit notes are never removed once issued; the link stays); message (mail rows are history; the link they carried keeps its own state) |
| Subscription | money, setting | billing | the plan a firm pays us for, its trial, limits and the month's issued-invoice count; our own GST invoices to the firm hang here | firm (never removed with the firm's plan; books stay at least 8 years whatever the plan state) |
| Template | content | comms | the look and wording of invoices, credit notes, receipts, quotes and their emails, with the firm's logo, colour, footer and terms | firm (templates go with the firm only after the 8 year book-keeping period); invoice (issued documents keep the template version they were printed with); message (mail rows keep the template version they were built from) |
| Time Entry | master | billing | hours a person spent for a customer; when approved they become invoice lines. MAYBE WAVE 1, the owner decides before the build (question Q5); every answer below is marked room until then | customer (blocked while any entry exists for the customer; archive the customer instead); invoice (a billed entry points at the invoice that holds its line; voiding the invoice or deleting its draft frees the entry); item (an entry keeps the task name it was saved with; an archived service stays on old entries); user (a person who leaves keeps their entries under their name) |
| User | person | core | one login per email for the whole product; a person who may belong to many firms | membership (a login is never erased while memberships exist; closing it ends every membership and the name stays on history); invite (invites belong to an email, not a login; they survive a closed login and cannot be accepted by it) |

## Risks to test first

| Assumption | Cheap test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Small service firms will leave Excel, Vyapar or free Zoho Invoice and pay Rs 499 a month for a GST invoice tool | put a priced fake-door page on a landing page and talk to 10 firm owners; count who gives a card or a firm yes | open |
| A UPI QR plus I-have-paid claims, with money recorded by hand, gets firms paid faster without a gateway | five firms use only QR for 30 days; compare days to payment with their last quarter | open |
| Owners will record payments themselves without a bank statement match | watch five firms for two weeks; count paid invoices that have a recorded payment against what the bank shows | open |
| Accountants accept registers, summaries and Tally XML without a GSTR-1 JSON file | show the month-end pack to 8 chartered accountants and ask which tool they would import it into | open |
| Per-line half-up rounding and the odd-paisa CGST plus SGST rule match what accountants' filing tools compute to the paisa | run 200 invoices of all rates through the portal's offline tool and a CA's software and diff every paisa | open |
| The fixed number format PREFIX/YY-YY/NNNNN is acceptable to most firms | ask 20 firms for their current invoice number and count how many it fits or can be mapped to | open |
| Ten free invoices a month is enough to hook a firm and tight enough to convert | ask 15 firms how many invoices they issue a month and find the median | open |
| Invoices sent from our domain reach the inbox at Gmail, Outlook, Zoho and Yahoo | send 100 seeded invoices with SPF, DKIM and DMARC and count spam placements | open |
| Chartered accountants will invite their clients because the accountant seat is free | offer the free seat to 10 CAs and count client firms invited in 30 days | open |
| Most firms are below Rs 5 crore so a fake e-invoice adapter is enough before launch | ask the 10 design-partner firms for their aggregate turnover band before the build | open |
| The 2025-Act section numbers, Form 131 and 168 names and the 1 November 2026 credit note change are right as printed | give the rule table to a chartered accountant for one hour before the build | open |
| One GSTIN, one bank account and one business per plan fits most firms of 1 to 20 people | ask 20 firms how many GSTINs and bank accounts they invoice from | open |
| Reminders at 1, 7 and 15 days after due date, one per customer, do not annoy payers or the firm | run them for 5 firms for a month and count complaints and payments within 3 days | open |
| A customer's UPI app can pay the full balance of a typical invoice, so the QR works above Rs 1 lakh | pay test amounts of Rs 1,00,001 and Rs 2,00,000 from three UPI apps to a business ID | open |
| Owners and accountants accept 24-hour delay on bank and UPI changes and a Waiting for IRN state without calling support | watch five design-partner firms for a month; count support tickets that mention a bank change or an unsent invoice | open |
| Money recorded on account with its real receipt date and a 'tax due in the receipt month' flag is enough for firms that take advances, before the receipt voucher exists | give the flag and the register line to 5 coaching or agency firms and ask their accountant to file one month from it | open |
