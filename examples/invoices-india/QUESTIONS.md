# Questions only the owner can answer (most costly first)

1. Take payments through a gateway (Razorpay link) in wave 1, or only a UPI QR and button with hand-recorded payments? Recommend QR and button plus "I have paid" claims; gateway in wave 2. Why: a gateway adds KYC, webhooks, refunds, fees and reconciliation, about 60 lines, and the QR already gets a firm paid.
2. Connect a real e-invoice provider in wave 1, or build the flow against the fake adapter and connect later? Recommend build the full flow, connect one real provider before launch only if a design-partner firm is above Rs 5 crore. Why: most 1 to 20 person firms are below the limit and a provider contract costs time and money.
3. Include advance receipts (receipt vouchers with GST on advances) in wave 1? Recommend wave 2 with room in the data; wave 1 refuses overpayment with a clear message. Why: GST on advances for services is real for coaching and agencies, but it is its own document, series and returns table.
4. Produce the GSTR-1 JSON file in wave 1, or only registers, summaries and an error list? Recommend registers and error list now, JSON in wave 2. Why: a wrong portal schema costs trust; accountants can use the registers meanwhile.
5. Time billing and expense re-billing in wave 1? Recommend wave 2; recurring drafts and manual lines serve a month of billing. Why: they are two modules, about 30 lines, and they pull in staff entry and approval rules.
6. Free plan limit, paid price and users per plan? Recommend 10 issued invoices a month free, one paid plan at Rs 499 a month or Rs 4,999 a year including GST, 5 users and one free accountant seat. Why: it fixes the limit counter, trial copy and our own GST invoices; change the numbers if your market data says otherwise.
7. Does one accountant login work across firms free of charge, and may the accountant invite themselves? Recommend free, one seat per firm, invited by the firm only. Why: it is the main growth path and it sets the multi-firm login design.
8. Sign-in by phone OTP in wave 1? Recommend no: email and password with optional TOTP. Why: SMS needs a registered sender and template, and SIM-swap rules follow.
9. Customer portal and accept-quote link in wave 1? Recommend no: a share link per document with UPI button and viewed times only. Why: a portal needs customer logins, a second tenancy view and dispute flows.
10. May the product send WhatsApp itself (Business API) in wave 1? Recommend no: click-to-chat with a prefilled message and the share link. Why: the API needs approved templates, opt-in storage and per-message cost.
